## Demand gate (run before every script; about 10 minutes)
1. Search YouTube for the *question type*, not your exact title, filtered to the past year and sorted by views: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=<words>&sp=CAMSBAgFEAE%253D`.
2. **Pass** if at least two unrelated channels under about 200K subscribers got 100K+ views with that question type in the past year.
3. **Clone check:** if a near-identical title appeared in the last 60 days, change the angle (a different place, year or problem), not just the wording.
4. Write the result at the top of the episode's `claims.md`.

Why: on Ink Explainer, wording and topic alone separated 39K-view videos from 800K-view ones, and on comparable channels "how humans invented X" titles got 1K–12K views while everyday-life questions got 100K to over 1M.
