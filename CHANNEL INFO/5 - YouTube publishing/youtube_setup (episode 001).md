What was daily life in medieval London really like? Spend one ordinary day in London in 1390 and see how working people lived in the Middle Ages, from the church bells before dawn to the curfew bell at night.

You share a straw mattress with three strangers, sign on as an apprentice for at least seven years with no marrying until it's done, find out what medieval people ate and drank, queue at a cookshop for a hot pie, meet the gong farmers who emptied the city's cesspits, and see what Londoners did for fun before the city gates shut.
