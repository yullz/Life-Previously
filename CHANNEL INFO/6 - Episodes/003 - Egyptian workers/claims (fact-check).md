## Checks and limits

Detailed refutation attempts and source access are in research/fact_checks.json. No separate-model review has happened or is required for this script under the owner's latest instruction. Do not represent the director's self-review as independent verification. Exact individual absence excuses, numerical leave entitlements, exact grain weights, modern wage conversions and 'first strike ever' were deliberately excluded.

The main frame is approximately1240BC. Evidence from other generations is introduced as separate community records; the RamessesIII protest has an explicit later transition. Illustrative workers and the imagined offer are reconstruction/framing, not new historical claims. Sources use different absolute chronologies for the protest; the script uses the regnal year and century.
