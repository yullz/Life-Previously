## Description draft
What would a job in ancient Egypt actually pay—and would you take it? Visit the royal tomb workers of Deir el-Medina to weigh grain pay, housing, support when ill, and the risk of provisions arriving late.

We begin around 1240 BC, during Ramesses II's reign. Separate records introduce other parts of the community's life; the later strike under Ramesses III is clearly dated. These specialists do not represent every Egyptian worker.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived, through illustrated reconstructions and surviving evidence.
