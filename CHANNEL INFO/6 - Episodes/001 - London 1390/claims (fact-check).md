## Myth to correct
- "Medieval people drank beer because water was deadly" (S032-S038).

## Contested points
- "Thrown out of the window": ordinances show it was illegal and sometimes happened; the script says exactly that.
- Exact meal times varied by class and season; the script says "around late morning".
- The night-work ban covered named trades, not every trade; the script says "many trades".

## Hook
- Stakes for the viewer: bells and daylight decide when you wake, work, eat and are allowed outside.
- Viewer's objection: "People just did what they wanted."
- Questions: When do you start work? What do you eat without a kitchen? What happens if you're out when the last bell rings? (held to the end)
