Fix the main narrative to London,July1944; no mash-up ration table. The family is invented as an illustrative household and explicitly labelled,not a filmed family or historical diary. Ordinary cheese2oz/week cross-checked againstJune1944Hansard and announcedOctoberrestoration. Never multiply adult milk by four or assume special cheese. Distinguish entitlements,money,retail availability and household labour. Read the whole July18Food/Shopping report so complaints are not cherry-picked over its recorded satisfaction. Officialfilm is discussed via catalogue only,no claim to have viewed it. Wooltonpie is a small source-bounded recipe demonstration; no exact disputed oatmeal spoon quantity,no modern luxury filling,no complete nutrition/weekcostpromise.
