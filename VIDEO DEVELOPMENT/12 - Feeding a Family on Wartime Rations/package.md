# Could You Feed a Family on Britain’s Wartime Rations?

## Alternate titles

- Your Ration Book Is Not Your Dinner

## Description

A ration book could protect your share of some foods,but it could not cook dinner. In London inJuly1944,follow an illustrative family's choices through the grocer,the queue and the kitchen. We separate rationed foods from scarce unrationed foods,build a period vegetable pie,and compare official promises with contemporary reports of everyday shopping.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
