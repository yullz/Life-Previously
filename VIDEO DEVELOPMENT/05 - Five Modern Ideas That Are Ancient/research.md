# Research and boundaries

Original countdown with a functional question: could the Visitor assemble familiar conveniences from ancient examples? Three archaeological stops precede two designs in Hero's text. This is an explicitly labelled multi-place format exception, not a single ancient city containing all five examples. Archaeological limits matter: Regio V does not justify a precise illustrated menu; Ostia's extant phases do not supply a named tenant or monthly price; Bath's modern Victorian terrace must never appear in the Roman reconstruction. Date Bath to the fourth-century complex and use c. AD 350 as the reconstruction convention. Hero is placed in Roman-era Alexandria, approximately first century AD, without claiming an exact invention date. Sections 21 and 37 have been read in full, with 38 checked to prevent mixing two different door designs. The narration follows described sequences, not a claim that a particular excavated device operated.
