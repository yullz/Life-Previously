## Why a farmer would want it
::: S025 | Simple nutrient dots move from closed bucket icon toward soil/root icon; no magical instant plant growth. [still]
Growing crops draws on nutrients in the soil. Human waste contains nutrients, including nitrogen and phosphorus, that can give it fertilizer value.

::: S026 | Farmer examines established crop rows beside covered storage, no chemical flask or modern agronomist equipment. [same-as: S003] [pan-left]
Buyers need no modern laboratory to notice results in their fields. Agricultural writings discuss the material's use and quality.

::: S027 | Small separate icons for plant compost, ash, animal manure container and night-soil container; no exact recipe. [still]
They discuss other materials too. Night soil is one fertilizer source, rather than the single secret behind all farming.

::: S028 | Farmer checks covered storage near simple shed; cultivated plots in background, no step-by-step application instructions. [same-as: S003] [pan-right]
Historical manuals consider collection, storage and application. This is knowledge and work, not simply disposal outside the city.

::: S029 | Town and field icons connected by two labelled arrows; no invented population or tonnage. [still]
The expanding city creates two connected opportunities: more people want food, and more people produce the material that farmers can use.

::: S030 | Edo vegetable seller displays clean daikon and greens; customers in ordinary clothes, no waste buckets anywhere near food. [pan-left]
Nearby growers have customers for fresh vegetables in town. The countryside, in turn, has customers for fertilizer.

::: S031 | Farmer and dealer discuss closed containers and blank ledger, no sniffing or exposed material. [pose: closeup-thoughtful 0.8,1.02 0.82] [zoom-out]
Quality matters too. Agricultural writers discuss differences in the material they obtain. Buying fertilizer is not simply buying the fullest possible container.

::: S032 | Two equal closed-container icons; one has added-water annotation, usefulness question remains, no unsupported numeric concentration. [still]
That explains the later argument about added water. A larger-looking load is not necessarily a more useful purchase.

::: S033 | Closed wooden bucket beside a simple locally drawn water-weight icon, no transparent contents or accurate percentage claim. [same-as: S006] [zoom-in]
First, it must reach the farm. Much of what the collector carries is water.

::: S034 | Collector on earth road between town edge and fields, no exact distance scale, load realistic. [pose: watch-arms-crossed 0.8,0.94 0.6] [same-as: S005] [pan-left]
Valuable in a field, expensive to move. Distance is part of the price.

::: S035 | Two closed buckets hang symmetrically from flexible pole on shoulder, feet firmly grounded, no crushing exaggerated burden. [same-as: S005] [zoom-out]
Our collector cannot turn its value into lighter buckets. Every step still has to move the weight that sits on his shoulder.

::: S036 | Simple road and waterway alternatives connect town to farms, marked schematic rather than geographic map. [still]
The route matters as much as the supply. This story is becoming a transport business.

## The weight of the business

::: S039 | Separate illustrative handcart with securely tied closed containers; collector walks beside it, no simultaneous shoulder load. [same-as: S005] [zoom-out]
Horses and small handcarts offer other ways to move loads. They still require handling, equipment and someone to do the travelling.

::: S041 | Dealer meets farmer at modest store, covered load behind; equal respectful human depiction, no universal wealth claim. [same-as: S031] [zoom-in]

::: S050 | Two separate boat silhouettes for inbound goods and outbound covered fertilizer, no shared cargo diagram. [still]

## Even this could be watered down
::: S061 | Non-graphic explanatory reconstruction of boat with closed cargo; separate blue droplet overlay indicates alleged dilution, no exposed contents. [note: Official complaint, May 1867 · reproduced in Koshigaya history] [same-as: S046] [zoom-in]
The eighteen sixty-seven notice describes dealers adding water during transport, increasing the apparent size of the load they could offer for sale.

::: S062 | Generic load blocks divided inconsistently between advertised whole and alleged extra; no invented exact price. [still]
It also complains about deceptive accounting for how much constituted a boatload, and charging more for what was presented as an extra portion.

::: S063 | Farmer examines closed delivered vessels beside field, no laboratory or failed crop attributed to an invented case. [pose: closeup-worried 0.8,1.02 0.82] [same-as: S003] [zoom-out]
Farmers could pay for a quantity that failed to deliver the expected fertilizer value. That is the conflict.

::: S064 | Short attributed paraphrase of enforcement provision, no execution/prison image or claim punishment occurred. [still]
The notice threatens punishment for dishonest sales. A related agreement calls for action against people seen adding water along the route.

::: S065 | Hands open plain ledger while covered load sits beyond; actual checked English label added locally, no generated Japanese writing. [same-as: S031] [zoom-in]
Transaction records and scheduled checks are required too. A dealer's assurance is not enough.

::: S066 | Separate labels complaint / rule / outcome unknown, sparse source card without dense paragraph. [still]
These are responses to a reported problem. They do not prove every trader cheated, or that enforcement always worked.

::: S067 | Farmer, dealer, closed containers and record table in one coherent modest setting, no caricature. [same-as: S031] [zoom-out]
But they prove the story is not simply about throwing something away. Buyers care about quality, quantity, price and access to a reliable supply.

::: S068 | Plain four-part local typography: quality, quantity, price, rights; closed bucket small secondary icon. [still]
An unusual product still raises ordinary market questions: who gets paid, and for what?

::: S069 | Five distinct role icons connected with optional pathways, no rigid universal hierarchy. [still]
Tenants, landlords, collectors, dealers and farmers can stand at different points in the exchange. Their interests need not match.

::: S070 | Collector secures load while farmer and dealer confer, practical scene rather than coins raining down. [same-as: S010] [pan-left]
The landlord wants a buyer. The farmer wants useful fertilizer. The transporter needs the journey to make economic sense.

::: S071 | Original courtyard after collection, doors and service area orderly but modest; no transformation into luxury housing. [same-as: S002] [zoom-out]

::: S072 | Closed vessel with restrained local educational marker separating value from sanitation; no hazard spectacle. [same-as: S006] [pan-right]
There is one more distinction to make before we call the system clever. A valuable fertilizer is not automatically a harmless substance.

## Useful does not mean harmless
::: S073 | Neutral microscopic-dot symbol beside closed bucket, clearly conceptual and not a diagnosis of historical person. [still]
Human waste can carry pathogens and parasite eggs. Turning it into an economic resource does not, by itself, make those risks disappear.

::: S074 | Source card identifying modern ministry explanation, no modern plant depicted as1867technology. [note: Japan Ministry of the Environment · historical recycling and sanitary treatment] [still]
Japan's environmental ministry distinguishes historical recycling from later sanitary treatment. They are related, but different, achievements.

::: S075 | Modest original courtyard and closed service area, no spotless glowing utopia or invented sick family. [same-as: S002] [zoom-out]
So we should not turn this episode into a claim that old Edo had solved every waste problem, or that reuse made illness impossible.

::: S076 | Broad network town↔multiplefarms with open branches, no single sealed circular loop or mass-balance percentage. [still]

::: S077 | Established vegetable plots and distant settlement, covered storage separate from produce, calm documentary framing. [same-as: S003] [zoom-in]

::: S078 | Small sequence of access, bucket, route, agreement and field icons; no new unverified mechanism. [still]
