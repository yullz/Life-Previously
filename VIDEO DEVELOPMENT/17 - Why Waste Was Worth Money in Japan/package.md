## Description

In Edo, the city that became Tokyo, human waste could be a valuable agricultural commodity. But the money did not necessarily go to the person who produced it.

Follow an illustrative1867 route from a shared privy to the farming villages outside the city, through landlord contracts, heavy transport and a documented problem with watered-down cargoes. This is a history of work and trade, with non-graphic illustrations.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
