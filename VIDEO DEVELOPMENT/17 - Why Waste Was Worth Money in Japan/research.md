# Research and boundaries

# Research direction

Edo and the eastern farming hinterland,1867. The story follows value rather than gross-out spectacle: whose waste is it, why buy it, who carries it, and why would anyone counterfeit a load? The concrete documentary payoff is the May1867 anti-dilution notice reproduced in Koshigaya's municipal history. The1845 price agreement is a clearly dated earlier example.

Tajima's2007 article supplies landlord rights, daytime collection, labour and transport constraints. Read passages distinguish Edo from Kanazawa; no Kanazawa rice payment is imported. Municipal histories independently support river transport, crop exchange and seasonal prices. Ministry material explains the nutrient value and the difference between reuse and sanitation.

Reject popular claims of universal perfect cleanliness, exact landlord fortunes, one fixed price for a poop, or everybody being paid personally. Do not use the ministry's modern-currency conversion or its undated historical price table. Later Meiji/Showa statistics and the museum bucket's broad19th–20th-century date stay visibly separate from1867.


## SRC01 — Kayo Tajima, The Marketing of Urban Human Waste in the Early Modern Edo/Tokyo Metropolitan Area (2007)

https://journals.openedition.org/eue/1039

Read: Paragraphs3–7,16–19,23 and35–43 read; introduction, landlord ownership, collection, transport and quoted early manuals. Later Meiji statistics not used.

Use/limits: Research article quoting older records. Kanazawa annual rice payments and distance rings are not Edo examples; exact capacities/ratios omitted. Collection could be daytime.

## SRC02 — Koshigaya municipal history: night soil, pp743–745

https://adeac.jp/koshigaya-city-digital-archives/texthtml/d000010/mp000010-100010/ht003190

Read: Full section read, including1845 agreement,1860 purchase example, May1867 notice and six provisions of implementing agreement.

Use/limits: Main documentary incident is1867. Earlier/later prices not imported into one transaction. Official allegation and prohibition do not prove every dealer cheated or enforcement succeeded.

## SRC03 — Katsushika municipal history: farming in Edo's eastern hinterland

https://www.city.katsushika.lg.jp/history/history/2-3-2-123-2.html

Read: Complete short section on crops, river transport, wealthy farmers' transport sideline and seasonal prices.

Use/limits: No exact boat type universal; no modern dates or surviving1960s vessels depicted as1867.

## SRC04 — Japan Ministry of the Environment, Night Soil Treatment and Decentralized Wastewater Treatment Systems
