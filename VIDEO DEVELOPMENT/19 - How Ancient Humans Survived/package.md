## Description

You are standing on a frozen plain 18,000 years ago with a hoodie and no plan. How did people get through a million years of nights like this? Six real camps, oldest first: a fire carried into Wonderwerk Cave a million years ago, wooden spears by a lake in Germany 300,000 years ago, a Neanderthal who lived for years with one arm and one good eye, a 39,600-year-old leather seam, the oldest bed of grass by the Sea of Galilee, and a house built from the bones of 37 mammoths. The answer at the end is not muscles.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
