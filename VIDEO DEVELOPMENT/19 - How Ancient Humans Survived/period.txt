A clearly labelled journey through six excavated camps, oldest first: Wonderwerk Cave, South Africa, about 1,000,000 years ago; Schöningen, Germany, about 300,000 years ago; Shanidar Cave, Iraq, about 50,000 years ago; Canyars near Barcelona, about 39,600 years ago; Ohalo II on the Sea of Galilee, about 23,000 years ago; Mezhyrich, Ukraine, about 18,000 years ago. The night-time frame is the Mezhyrich steppe. Never merge the stops into one place or one people. People are shown respectfully as skilled adults and children of their time: dark-skinned lean early humans at Wonderwerk (hide wraps are an illustrative modesty choice, not a claim), robust Neanderthals with large noses and hide clothing at Shanidar, Homo sapiens in sewn hide clothing at the later stops. Tools are wood, stone, bone, antler and ivory only: no metal, no pottery, no bows, no domestic animals, no fields. Fire is small and tended. Every date on screen is the site's own, not a modern publication year.

Open-air camp on a low terrace with the Mediterranean a grey line at the horizon, pine scrub, a small fire; two Homo sapiens adults in sewn hide clothing working hides: one presses a pointed stone tool through a hide laid over a flat bone, another stretches a hide on pegs. ohalo_camp: Ohalo II, south-western shore of the Sea of Galilee, Israel, about 23,000 years ago; fisher-hunter-gatherer camp; reconstruction. Lakeshore with several low oval brush huts of tamarisk, willow and oak branches covered with smaller branches, leaves and grass, open-air hearths, drying fish on sticks, a few adults in hide and plant-fibre clothing; low hills behind, calm water at left. No pottery, no boats, no fields. Clear right foreground.
