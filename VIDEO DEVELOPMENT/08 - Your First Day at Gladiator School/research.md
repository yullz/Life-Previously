# Research and boundaries

The hook is an imagined first day whose first opponent is a wooden post. Route: controlled gate, status, equipment and practice, sleeping rooms, heated hall/baths, public arena. c.AD200 is a broad Imperial narrative setting, not the date of a preserved diary or exact construction phase. Carnuntum supplies spatial constraints; museum objects and Seneca supply separately labelled comparative evidence. Original research compared the current site account's qualified wording with the more confident2014 paper and VIAS summary. Keep the more careful wording. Read the underlying lamp record: the correct training lamp is74.51.2030, not the different gallery link's74.51.1845. The latter depicts defeated combatants, so using it as training proof would be wrong. Reject Ephesus ash-drink/vegetarian headlines as a local meal plan; reject exact75 occupants, medical room and underground punishment cell as certainties. Heated hall can be assembly or training. No staged death, first-day duel or guaranteed survival. The story's emotional shift is from fear of an opponent to recognition of institutional control. Original illustrations, not copied research reconstructions; room plans establish geography, elevations remain illustrative.
