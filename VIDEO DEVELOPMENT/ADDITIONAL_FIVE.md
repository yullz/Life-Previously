# Five additional director selections

Selected by Astra under the owner's9October expansion to fifteen. These offer everyday questions a viewer can understand without prior history knowledge. Audience appeal is an editorial judgment, not measured demand or a promise of views. Dates and numerical claims will be locked from full source passages during development.

| Title | Click promise and thumbnail direction | Evidence route and boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Who Woke You Up Before You Had an Alarm Clock? | A paid wake-up call delivered at your window. Visitor jolted awake, period knocker's pole outside; PAY TO WAKE UP? | Original research in Journal of Victorian Culture,25(3),331ff: https://academic.oup.com/jvc/article/25/3/331/5829726. Follow its cited contemporary records. Alarm clocks already existed; never claim the occupation predates their invention. Avoid the obvious recycled question being answered with an invented who-woke-the-knocker chain. |
| Could You Feed a Family on Britain’s Wartime Rations? | Plan a real meal within a documented allowance. Visitor beside modest ingredients and a locally typeset ration card; FOR A WEEK? | IWM's1944 Rationing in Britain record: https://film.iwmcollections.org.uk/record/800 and National Archives contextualisation: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/education/resources/home-front-1939-1945-film-sources-2/. Lock a specific month and household; propaganda is not proof of universal lived experience. The thumbnail must depict exactly the covered rationed items, not falsely imply that is all food allowed. |
| You Move to Australia in1854. What Is the Voyage Like? | Food, space, boredom and the inability to get off halfway. Visitor squeezed beside a period berth; STILL AT SEA. | Marco Polo Chronicle,1854 voyage, held by State Library Victoria: https://blogs.slv.vic.gov.au/such-was-life/liverpool-to-melbourne-the-newspaper/. Distinguish1854 voyage from the ship's famous1852 passage. Read the actual shipboard newspaper; do not turn advertised provisions into equal passenger menus. |
| When Beaver Fur Was Money: What Could You Buy? | A familiar shopping problem with a surprising unit of account. Visitor beside a pelt and one researched trade item; PAY IN FUR? | Archives of Manitoba defines made beaver: https://www.gov.mb.ca/chc/archives/hbca/glossaries.html. York Factory1720 record-based exhibition route: https://mhs.mb.ca/docs/dawsonandhind/dh062.pdf. Lock the post/year and actual exchange table before naming a price. Explain Indigenous agency and colonial power; no generic costume or barter-with-no-rules myth. |
| Why Did Grocery Stores Make You Serve Yourself? | Recognisable supermarket habits that once needed explaining. Visitor at a period turnstile holding an approved basket variant only if needed; SERVE YOURSELF? | Tennessee State Museum: https://tnmuseum.org/junior-curators/posts/the-story-of-piggly-wiggly-the-first-supermarket; contemporary Memphis-area photograph c1918: https://www.loc.gov/resource/cph.3b37546/. Patent and period advertisements will establish the mechanism. Do not repeat first-supermarket-ever claims without defining the category; no modern trolleys, barcode scanners or an unsupported savings percentage. |

The UK worker/rationing stories, Australian migration, Canadian fur economy and US shopping change broaden settings while keeping the channel's ordinary-life focus. Publication order should separate repeated centuries and topics; no calendar is assigned here.
