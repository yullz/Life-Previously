## Description

Step into a reconstructed Memphis grocery store in1917, built around Clarence Saunders's self-serving-store patent. Customers picked their own goods—but the route, the baskets and the staffed checkout were carefully planned. Original1916launch advertisements reveal what shoppers were being offered, what work shifted to them and which service disappeared. The customer and shopping list are illustrative; design claims are distinguished from verified results.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
