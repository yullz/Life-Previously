## Description

A tap at the bedroom window could be somebody's paid job. Follow the people who woke industrial workers,then meet a woman whose account describes the prices,customers and awkward question behind the trade: who woke her? Our starting point is Manchester in1862; Mrs Waters' story comes from an1877 account that leaves her northern-English town unnamed.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
