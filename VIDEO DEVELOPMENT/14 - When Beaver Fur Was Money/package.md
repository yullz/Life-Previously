## Description

At Fort Albany on James Bay in1733, a trade list valued fishhooks, clothing, kettles and sugar in beaver units. But a pelt was not a fixed-price shopping voucher. We follow an imagined visit to explore the real prices, the importance of quality, and the relationships behind the trade. Prices follow the1733schedule printed by Arthur Dobbs in1744; the customer and six-unit shopping example are illustrative.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
