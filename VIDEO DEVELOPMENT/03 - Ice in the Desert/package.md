## Description

A hot summer drink begins with a cold winter night. At Meybod in Iran, follow the seasonal work behind the traditional yakhchal: shallow freezing ponds, a carefully placed shade wall, harvested ice and an insulated store. This illustrated reconstruction explains how people kept winter ice for summer—and what the buildings could not do.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
