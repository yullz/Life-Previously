## The same ship, different lives

::: S014 | Modestfirst-cabinsaloon,fixedtableandfewwell-dressedadults,clearlynotgrandlinerdiningroom. [note: Illustrative first-cabin area] [pan-left]

::: S015 | Samebunkroomwithseveraladultpassengers,smalltrunkssecured,nofantasizedexactplan. [note: Period reconstruction · exact layout unknown] [same-as: S010] [zoom-out]

## Wash, eat, repeat
::: S025 | Twoadults washplainclothesinbasins,shortsecuredlineawayfromrigging,calmdaylight. [pose: look-around 0.8,0.94 0.6] [note: Chronicle,5August1854] [zoom-in]
Begin with washing. An early August notice sets aside Mondays and Thursdays, with the articles in use cleared before eleven.

::: S026 | Passengerremovesdampclothingfromlinewhilecrewkeepsclearpathnearhatch; no inventedpunishment. [same-as: S025] [pan-left]
Your laundry is now part of a shared timetable. You cannot treat the working deck as your private back garden.

::: S027 | Ordinarydoctorwelcomesadultpatienttocotroom,plainwaterjug,nosurgeryormoderninstruments. [note: 11amand4pm · emergency exceptions] [zoom-out]

::: S028 | Deckwithsmallgroupnearhospitaldoorandothersmovingpast,notgiantqueueorsickcrowd. [same-as: S003] [pan-right]

::: S029 | Securedbarrels,sacks andpreservedfoodboxes,woodenbeams; noinventedprintedlabels. [pose: watch-arms-crossed 0.8,0.94 0.6] [note: Chronicle,9September1854,Provisions] [zoom-in]
Food travels with the ship too. Its inventory lists flour, bread, rice, oatmeal, beef, pork and preserved foods.

::: S030 | Smallsecurepenswithfewsheepandpigs,separatepoultrycrate,no looseanimalstampede. [pan-left]

::: S031 | Whole-shipstoresiconclearlyseparatefromoneplate; noportionmath. [note: Inventory≠individualration] [still]

::: S032 | Cookinordinaryaproncheckssecuredsackbesideplaincontainers,notluxurychefhat. [same-as: S029] [pan-right]

::: S033 | Ordinaryadultsseatedatsmallsecuredmealboardwithplainfood,notinventedexactmenu. [same-as: S010] [zoom-in]
For a passenger, meals also mark time. Another dinner is something to do when the horizon has offered very little news.

::: S034 | Adultpausesatplainmeal,anothernearby quietlyeats,Visitorlistensfromright. [pose: closeup-thoughtful 0.8,1.02 0.82] [same-as: S010] [still]

::: S035 | Quietpassengersleaning orseatedproperlyondeckbench,longhorizon,normalboredfaces. [same-as: S003] [zoom-out]
A supplied ship can still become tiresome. The problem is not always hunger, fear, or spectacular disaster.

::: S036 | Beginningcalmphase,shiponlowswell,sailslessfilled,nobigbowwake. [note: Calm periods reported in the Chronicle] [same-as: S002] [pan-right]

## Good weather can be bad news

In August, the paper describes a calm after a fancy-dress ball. The ship rises and falls with the swell but makes little progress.

::: S038 | Adultstareatcalmhorizon,riggingstill,safehandonrail,no identicalclonefaces. [same-as: S003] [pan-left]

## Rules, care, and consequences

## The paper runs into the timetable
::: S073 | Printerchecksremainingpaperstackatcompactpress,lesspaperthanearlyanchor; nottotallyempty. [same-as: S018] [zoom-in]

::: S074 | Tenlocallydrawnsmallbundleswithsimplecount,notmodeldrawnlabels; one highlightedfinalportion. [note: Chronicle,14October1854,LastoftheChronicles] [still]

::: S075 | Ninecompletedblankissuebundlesandoneunusedpaperportion shownschematicallylocaloverlay,notexactpaperweights. [same-as: S018] [zoom-out]

## The crossing ends. The move does not.
::: S085 | Sameshipatbayanchorage,smallsailcraftandlowshore; no literallydockedriverclaim. [same-as: S084] [zoom-in]
The Marco Polo reached Melbourne in late October, after leaving Liverpool in July. The promise of departure had become an arrival.

::: S086 | SimplelocalPortPhillip/YarraregionalmapwithKulinCountrylabel,noexacttraditionalboundariesinvented. [note: MuseumsVictoria,TheFoundingofMelbourne] [still]

::: S087 | Bayandlandscapeviewwithcolonialbuildings,notfantasyuntouchedemptycontinent; no inventedconfrontation. [same-as: S084] [zoom-out]
European settlement had displaced First Peoples and restricted their access to land and resources. A passenger's fresh start belonged within that larger history.
