## Description

Before a new life inAustralia came weeks of sharing a sailing ship with strangers. We follow theMarcoPolo's1854voyage through its own onboardnewspaper: washingdays,foodstores,entertainment,rules,andwinds that refused to match the timetable. This isCaptainWilliamWild'svoyage,not the ship's earlierrecord run.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
