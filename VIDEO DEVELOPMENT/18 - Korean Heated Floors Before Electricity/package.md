## Description

Step into an illustrative Seoul home around 1900 and follow a kitchen fire beneath the floor. Traditional ondol warmed stone slabs, reshaped daily life and made the floor a place to sit, eat and sleep. We trace the smoke route, the work behind the warmth, and the wooden hall that offered a different answer in summer.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
