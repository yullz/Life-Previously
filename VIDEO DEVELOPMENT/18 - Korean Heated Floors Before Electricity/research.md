# Research and boundaries

Explain one cold-weather visit through a single consistent house. The historical engine is the combustion path, thermal storage and floor-level daily life; the narrative engine is the Visitor discovering that a kitchen fire changes where he eats, sits and sleeps. The house and host are illustrative. Distinguish source evidence from explanatory engineering inference. National-history English outreach contains exaggerations and a class-translation problem: use the more cautious Korean commentary for historical claims. Bishop supplies contemporary material and fuel observations but is a biased outsider, not a neutral authority on Korean society. Her hot temple cell remains a labelled aside, not this household. No spectacular invented duration, free-energy claim or construction tutorial.
