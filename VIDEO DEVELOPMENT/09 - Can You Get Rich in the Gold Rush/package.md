## Description

You have reached California's goldfields in1849. Do you take paid work, buy equipment, or chase a better claim? Following Kimball Webster's account on the Feather River, we track the gap between finding gold and keeping money—from a promising spell to rain, food bills and a change of plan. Brief comparisons visit Mokelumne and San Francisco; the Visitor's choices are illustrative.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
