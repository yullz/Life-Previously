## Description

A late meal, a demanding restaurant order, a performance—and people still working as dawn approaches. Join an imagined night out in Kaifeng around 1120, guided by Meng Yuanlao's memories of the Northern Song capital.

We distinguish the memoir's descriptions from our reconstructed route and show why a lively night economy did not mean everyone shared the same comfortable life.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
