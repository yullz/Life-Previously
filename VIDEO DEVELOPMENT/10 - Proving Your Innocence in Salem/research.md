# Research and boundaries

Test imagined defenses against actual cases,without a reliable-survival formula. Warren's examination shows innocence denied and reported fits interpreted through accusation. Nurse's neighbours offered character evidence;jury initially acquitted then reconsidered,with ambiguous wording important to Fisk;Nurse said she meant fellow prisoners and explained hearing difficulty. Wardwell confessed and recanted,saying he expected death either way. Esty/Cloyce requested help,witnesses and corroboration;Esty later urged separate examination and testing confessions. No supernatural event authenticated by an accusation. No diagnosis of ergotism,hysteria or universal deceit. No burning,magical thumbnail,torture reenactment or guaranteed strategy. Dates and Village/Town stages distinct. PEM timeline ending read and compared with Sarah Cloyce's three rejected indictments: later1693 outcome,not an automatic release in1692. Initial/final exact-script reviews still pending.
