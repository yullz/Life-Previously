## What Mary Easty asked them to test
::: S073 | Two separated interview symbols, clearly REQUESTED METHOD rather than event. [still]
Easty asked that the afflicted accusers be examined closely and kept apart for a time. Their accounts should be tested, not simply repeated.

::: S074 | Document foreground and Mary looking toward it, no named confessor added. [note: TEST THE CONFESSIONS] [same-as: S008] [pan-left]
She also urged scrutiny of confessions. She believed some people had falsely accused themselves and others, and that examination could reveal it.

::: S075 | Mary by window, thoughtful expression, no omniscient montage. [same-as: S008] [zoom-out]
Distinguish her belief from a complete explanation. She did not know every person's private history.

::: S076 | Accusation and confession cards share an unverified statement; no checkmark until independent support. [still]
But the proposal challenges an important assumption: repeating an accusation in a confession does not automatically make it reliable evidence.

::: S077 | Blank petition on table, Mary holds hands together, no religious apparition. [pose: closeup-listening 0.8,1.02 0.82] [same-as: S008] [zoom-in]

::: S078 | Folded cloth and plain bench, window light, no Halloween prop. [same-as: S023] [pan-left]
