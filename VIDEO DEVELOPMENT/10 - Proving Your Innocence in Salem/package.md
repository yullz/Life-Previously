## Description

If you were accused in Salem in1692,what could you say in your defense? We follow real examination records,neighbours' petitions,a disputed verdict and a condemned woman's plea for better evidence. The Visitor's questions are imagined;cases and sources are identified. Supernatural claims are not presented as facts.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
