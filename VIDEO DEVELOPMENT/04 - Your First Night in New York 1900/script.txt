## A cheap room can still be full

::: S027 | Plain coins beside closed unnumbered key on neutral field, no booking confirmation. [zoom-out]

::: S028 | Evening small room, bag at bed foot, modest window darkening, no lamps invented. [pose: watch-arms-crossed 0.8,0.94 0.6] [same-as: S012] [pan-right]

## The cheaper apartment is upstairs

::: S050 | Lower landing with two plain apartment doors, ordinary stairs rising left, one adult carrying a basket up; no readable numbers painted. [pose: count-fingers 0.8,0.94 0.6] [note: Veiller block study • 1 January 1900 • 39 tenements • 605 apartments] [same-as: S047] [pan-left]
