## Dinner goes out to be cooked

Mercier describes small households sending meat to pastry-shop ovens. Buying the food did not mean owning the best place to cook it.

::: S066 | Modestly dressed customer and baker at counter, no wealth indicators or starvation exaggeration. [same-as: S033] [pan-left]

::: S067 | Covered household dish on bench beside oven peel; no name or numbered ticket. [same-as: S033] [zoom-out]
