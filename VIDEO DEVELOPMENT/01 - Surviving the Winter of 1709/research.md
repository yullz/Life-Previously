# Research and boundaries

Scope: a clearly imaginary Visitor household in Paris, January1709, followed through changing seasons. The historical claims come from weather observations, an attributed memoir and a historian's synthesis. The story tests access to warmth, food and help; it does not invent an exact poor family's menu, income, fuel price or diary. Cross-place detour to Versailles is named aloud and on screen. Do not use Saint-Simon's all-crops/all-trees language literally. His accusations against officials are contested political testimony; the script treats distrust as part of the situation, not proof of a centrally planned famine. An apparent1709 relief illustration led to the museum's1693 catalogue record; it is excluded as1709 evidence. No unsupported coldest-ever claim, fixed national death total, period Celsius instrument, modern baguette or winter snow in the August event. Audience appeal is an editorial hypothesis, not measured demand or a viral forecast. The owner commissioned development of this title; launch-time packaging checks remain separate.
