Paris and its surrounding French countryside, January through August 1709, during Louis XIV's reign. Modest plastered masonry houses with steep slate or tile roofs, small wooden-framed windows and shutters; narrow pre-Haussmann streets. Interiors have a masonry hearth, wooden table and simple earthenware. Adult men wear plain long coats, waistcoats, knee breeches and stockings; adult women plain bodices, long petticoats, aprons and linen caps. Muted clothing. Winter snow belongs only in explicitly winter scenes; spring and August scenes are green/bare-ground as appropriate. Reconstructed ordinary settings, not exact recovered rooms.
