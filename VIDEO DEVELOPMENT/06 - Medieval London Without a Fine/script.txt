## Leave the sword behind

## The price of winning an argument

::: S027 | Knife partly out of scabbard resting on bench, no hand or threatened person. [note: Selected ordinance • drawing without striking] [same-as: S010] [zoom-out]
Under this particular London ordinance, drawing a sword or knife can be punishable even if you never land a blow.

::: S028 | Two clearly separated options with OR in centre; weapon icon safely above. [still]

::: S029 | Same two options; emphasize OR, no addition sign. [still]
The word 'or' matters. We are not automatically adding both punishments together.

::: S030 | Simple closed scabbard icon and two penalty options; no blood or wound. [still]

::: S031 | Both locals lower hands slightly; no punch staged, tension in posture only. [pose: watch-arms-crossed 0.8,0.94 0.6] [same-as: S025] [zoom-out]

::: S032 | Two rows, NO BLOOD / BLOOD; each displays money OR days; small fist icon, no injury. [still]
