## Did pirates have a bedtime?
::: S073 | One covered candle lantern with wick just extinguished on separate Roberts deck; dark sea beyond, no modern clock. [pose: closeup-listening 0.8,1.02 0.82] [note: Roberts’s crew • account published 1724] [same-as: S071] [zoom-in]
Did pirates have a bedtime? One account gives Bartholomew Roberts's crew a rule requiring lights and candles out at eight.

::: S074 | Two adults in plain shirts sit separately on open deck left with modest plain cups, no rowdy spectacle. [same-as: S071] [pan-left]
Anyone still inclined to drink afterward was supposed to do it on the open deck. Your evening came with conditions.

::: S075 | Simple blank card and dice icons with a crossed-out coin beside them; no readable playing card faces. [note: Roberts's reported articles · published 1724] [still]
The same account bans gambling at cards or dice for money. A pirate reputation did not automatically mean an absence of rules.

::: S076 | Unlit covered lantern and empty plain cup on left, no locals. [pose: closeup-listening 0.8,1.02 0.82] [same-as: S071] [still]

## A book where you least expect it
::: S085 | Cannon chamber schematic leads to paper fragments panel; exact count label composited locally. [still]
Paper survived inside a cannon chamber recovered from Queen Anne's Revenge. Conservators found sixteen tiny fragments and began working out what they were.

::: S086 | Small fragment beside blank closed book, no simulated original text. [pose: closeup-thoughtful 0.8,1.02 0.82] [note: Edward Cooke • voyage narrative, 1712] [same-as: S081] [pan-left]
Some printed words were still readable. Researchers identified them as part of a seventeen-twelve edition of a voyage narrative by Edward Cooke.

::: S087 | Locally typeset shortened book identification and simple globe icon, no fictional route line. [still]
It was an account of travel around the world. A book about voyages had itself ended up aboard this very eventful ship.

::: S088 | Fragment alone enlarged on parchment, no reader or reading group. [same-as: S081] [pan-right]

::: S089 | Fragment with plain book far behind, no submerged pristine whole volume. [same-as: S081] [zoom-in]
