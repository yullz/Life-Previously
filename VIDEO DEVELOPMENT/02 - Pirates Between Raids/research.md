# Research and boundaries

Anchor: Queen Anne's Revenge in early 1718, with an explicitly imagined workday. One dated comparison concerns Roberts's different crew, 1719–1722, as described in 1724. The script follows practical needs, then changes the viewer's expectation of freedom by naming a forcibly retained cook. Archaeology is not a daily diary: food bones can predate capture, a medicine tool cannot certify a cure, printed scraps cannot identify a reader. Rejected: a precise daily menu, a definite sail needle from the disputed silver object, pirate democracies as universally egalitarian, universal bedtime rules, book-club scenes, burying treasure, walking planks, and detailed vessel geometry unsupported by a plan. Johnson is cited under its published author's name; attribution to Daniel Defoe is disputed. Visual artifact references must be inspected before exact object drawing, but all narrative claims above use passages already read.
