## Description

A pirate ship still needed meals, repairs, navigation and medical care. Step aboard an illustrated reconstruction of Blackbeard’s Queen Anne’s Revenge and follow the work between raids, using objects recovered from the wreck and contemporary accounts. Then meet a cook who did not choose to join—and a book surviving where you would least expect it.

Life, Previously explores how ordinary people actually lived. Historical scenes are illustrated reconstructions; specific limits and evidence are explained in the episode.
